# Games24x7

> Games24x7 is a Mumbai-headquartered Indian online skill-gaming company founded in 2006
> by Bhavin Pandya and Trivikraman Thampy. It operates consumer gaming platforms —
> RummyCircle (online rummy), My11Circle (fantasy cricket, football, kabaddi and ludo)
> and Wowzy (a three-dice ludo variant). It is a business-to-consumer operator, not an
> API provider: as of 2026-08-16 it publishes no developer portal, no API reference, no
> machine-readable specification (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, MCP), no SDK for any
> service of its own, and no /.well-known/ discovery surface.
